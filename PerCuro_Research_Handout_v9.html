How the research partnership works

PerCuro works with physician practices to establish or support a research operation around the studies they want to conduct.

The arrangement brings together the investigator, qualified local staff and the operational support needed for the agreed work. Study activities may take place at the physician’s practice, a PerCuro infusion clinic or another agreed location, depending on the protocol and available resources.

Local staff may come from the existing clinic team, PerCuro or an agreed combination. Research coordination and specialist support can be shared across locations where the work allows it.

The investigator retains clinical decisions and oversight. The partnership sets out which work PerCuro takes on, what the practice retains, where activities occur and how staffing and costs are handled.

Choose the support you need

Full-service research support

PerCuro takes responsibility for coordinating the agreed research operation, including arranging the required local delivery and supporting work. The agreement identifies the practice’s retained duties.

Selected research services

PerCuro completes specific work alongside an existing operation. The clinic can start with one task, one study or a period of support.

These are two scopes within the same approach, not different operating models. Nursing, remote support and staffing arrangements are choices within either scope.

Services

Seven service areas. Each one says whether PerCuro does the work, coordinates work done by others, or advises, and gives an example of what the practice receives.

Study opportunities and feasibility

Preparation of an agreed ethics submission, with review and submission responsibilities identified.

Ongoing coordination

Take ownership of identified recurring tasks: scheduling, essential documents, data entry, query follow-up and monitoring preparation.

A written split of recurring tasks showing which PerCuro owns, with schedules, essential documents, data entry, queries and monitoring preparation kept current.

Questions from clinics

Can you help if we already have a coordinator?

Yes. Selected research services are designed for that situation. Your coordinator remains the practice’s lead, and PerCuro takes on defined tasks such as data entry, query follow-up, regulatory documents or monitoring preparation. Which tasks move, and how handovers work, are agreed at the start.

How much investigator time is needed?

Investigator time is concentrated on the parts only the investigator can do: eligibility and clinical decisions, safety review, and required review and sign-off. The agreement identifies what the investigator retains, and actual workload is reviewed once the study is running.

Who provides staff and covers absences?

Staff may come from your existing team, from PerCuro or an agreed combination. The staffing plan sets out who does what and the backup arrangements for absence, and coordination can be shared across locations where the work allows it.

Where will visits take place?

At your practice, at a PerCuro infusion clinic or at another agreed location, depending on the protocol and the resources available. The location is settled when the work is defined.

How are fees and start-up costs agreed?

Fees and any start-up costs are set out in the agreement before work begins, based on the agreed scope, staffing and locations. Selected services can be agreed for a single task, study or period of support.

Can we begin with one task or one study?

Yes. Many practices start with a single service or a single study and extend the arrangement later. Additional work is added by agreement.

For sponsors and CROs

Investigator sites with a relevant patient population, local visit delivery and coordinated site operations, assessed study by study.

Site experience

Investigator experience, therapeutic background and the local team are described for each site as it is confirmed. PerCuro’s specialty-care history covers rheumatology, inflammatory bowel disease, immunology, neurology and oncology; research experience is confirmed per site and per protocol.

Feasibility

A feasibility discussion connects what the protocol requires with what a specific site can deliver. Useful to bring: therapeutic area, phase and population; recruitment pathway and eligibility; visit schedule, procedures and nursing needs; geography, equipment and specimen requirements; activation timing.

Capacity

Capacity, recruitment assumptions and timelines are assessed for each study against investigator availability, staff, space and the patient population. Enrolment volumes and activation dates are not promised in advance.

Roles

The investigator retains clinical oversight and sign-off. Sponsor systems remain sponsor-controlled. PerCuro coordinates the agreed operational work and local delivery.
